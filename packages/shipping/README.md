# Shopper Shipping

The shipping layer of [Shopper](https://laravelshopper.dev): the carrier driver contract and
manager, the manual driver with zone rates, shipments and their tracking timeline, and the
scheduled tracking refresh. Carriers such as [`shopper/ups`](https://github.com/shopperlabs/ups),
[`shopper/fedex`](https://github.com/shopperlabs/fedex) and [`shopper/usps`](https://github.com/shopperlabs/usps)
register their driver on it.

This package is installed with [`shopper/framework`](https://github.com/shopperlabs/shopper).
It is not meant to be required on its own.

Read the [documentation](https://docs.laravelshopper.dev) to learn how Shopper works.
